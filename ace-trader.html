ACE Trader

A paper-trading research tool — find the trade, debate it, prove it on paper first.

← All projects

ACE Trader scans the market every half hour, scores opportunities against eleven strategies, and runs the best ideas through a small team of AI agents — scout, chart reader, skeptic, sizer — before anything is considered.

Every order is paper money, approved by a person first. The goal is to prove a strategy works on paper before a single real dollar is ever at stake.
