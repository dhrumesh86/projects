Please read — honest notes

What this Phase 1 app is — and is not

  • Rexy teaches with built-in lessons written for this app — original stories and questions, not copied from IXL, TVO, K5 or Khan Academy.
  • In-page helper — built-in Rexy tutor, not live Muse: tap the floating helper avatar (bottom-right) or any In-page helper button and the helper slides open over this webpage. Its replies come from built-in Rexy tutor logic on this page — it is not a live Muse connection. It can explain topics, give hints, set practice questions and check answers right there. For live Muse, tap Open live Muse chat inside the panel — that is the live Muse chat outside this app and opens muse.ai in a new tab. Page Help (top menu) is separate built-in FAQs. The tutor on the dedicated PC is still being built for saved progress and voice.
  • Read-aloud uses your browser's voice (SpeechSynthesis). If your browser has no voice, the words stay on screen to read together.
  • Voice answering: Checking your browser…
  • Curriculum: Topics follow Ontario Grade 2 and JK themes. Official expectation codes are unverified in this build, so none are shown as official.
  • Code Quest Level 1 teaches block thinking — sequencing, loops, events, variables — with original projects and exact, repeatable checking (final position, drawn picture, action counts, story order). Saved coding projects are kept on this device for this visit only, like all progress here, and clear when the page closes.
  • No PDFs as the main format, no ads, no leaderboards, and no comparing the brothers.
