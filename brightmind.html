BrightMind AI

A personal AI tutor for kids, built around the Ontario curriculum.

← All projects

BrightMind is a tutor, not an answer machine. It talks a child through a question step by step — a hint, a small step, a try, then an explanation — and only moves on when the child can explain it back.

It runs first on a dedicated home PC, with a cloud version rolling out gradually. Practice, games, workbooks and a parent dashboard are all part of it.
